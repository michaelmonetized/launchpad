# Launchpad - Development Plan

## Project Overview

Launchpad is a project management and client dashboard tool for Hustle Launch clients to track their projects, access deliverables, and communicate with the team.

- ✅ Next.js project setup
- ✅ Package.json configured

### Not Started

## Improvement Opportunities (Updated 2025-01-08)

### 🔴 Critical (MVP Features)

1. **Client Onboarding** - Invite clients to access their projects

2. **Project Dashboard** - Overview of all active projects

3. **Milestone Tracking** - Visual timeline with progress

4. **File Delivery** - Upload and share deliverables

5. **Notifications** - Email alerts for project updates

### 🟡 High Priority (Client Value)

6. **Comments/Feedback** - Threaded discussions on deliverables

7. **Approval Workflow** - Client sign-off on milestones

8. **Billing Portal** - View invoices, payment history

9. **Support Tickets** - Request help or changes

10. **Brand Assets** - Store logos, colors, fonts

11. **Calendar View** - Upcoming deadlines and meetings

12. **Meeting Scheduling** - Book calls with team

### 🟢 Nice to Have (Team Features)

13. **Internal Dashboard** - Team view of all clients

14. **Time Tracking** - Track time on projects

15. **Resource Library** - Templates, guides for clients

16. **Client Portal Customization** - White-label option

17. **Reporting** - Generate client reports

18. **Integrations** - Slack, email, calendar sync

### 🔧 Technical Requirements

19. **Multi-tenant** - Separate client data properly

20. **Role-based Access** - Client vs. team permissions

21. **File Storage** - Convex or Cloudflare R2

22. **Real-time Updates** - Convex subscriptions

23. **Email Notifications** - Resend for alerts

24. **Mobile Responsive** - Full mobile experience
